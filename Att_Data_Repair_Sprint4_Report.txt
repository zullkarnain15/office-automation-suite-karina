22. INTEGRASI RUN_JOB

Urutan run_job Sprint 4:
1. Validate request.
2. Validate output root.
3. Source workbook preflight/read sebelum job folder.
4. Reserve job folder.
5. Start Process.log.
6. Analyze source.
7. Determine status.
8. Generate TXT bila ada Final Records.
9. Generate Excel report.
10. Attach ReportArtifact to job result.
11. Write summary.json final.
12. Log final status.
13. Return AttDataRepairJobResult.

NO_VALID_RECORDS:
- Tidak membuat TXT kosong.
- Tetap membuat Excel report audit.
- summary.json report.generated true jika report berhasil.

Failure report:
- Job menjadi FAILED.
- TXT final yang sudah berhasil tidak dihapus.
- Failed summary mencatat report.generated false dan reason error.
